## Overview

This project is a full model of a power distribution system, built by our PES chapter. One part of it is a model AC generator that acts as the system's power plant. I volunteered to lead the generator team, and it turned into a real lesson in project management.

I came in with a general idea of what the generator should be and a plan to track our meetings and progress. In the first two weeks, though, I fell into the trap of taking on large parts of the work myself, including the circuit design, research, math, and generator visuals. I realized this was slowing us down and went against the point of leading a collaborative project, so I started delegating tasks and sections to my teammates.

## My role

From then on I focused on leading the project:

- Set the design goal of 20 W at 60 Hz and the project timeline
- Ran our meetings, kept our records up to date, and gave progress updates to the other project teams
- Reviewed and approved designs
- Worked with the team to set the number of phases and magnets, the number of coils, and turns per coil

I also designed parts of the 3D-printed generator with two of my teammates.

## Status

We finished the main generator body design by the end of the Spring 2026 semester and spent most of Fall 2026 designing the gear system. Our final test is planned for the last week of the semester.
